## Description: <br>
Xiaozhi English Grammar Coach helps learners identify recurring English grammar weaknesses and practice corrections through Socratic questioning instead of direct answers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External learners and education-focused agents use this skill to analyze English grammar errors, guide practice for tense, subject-verb agreement, prepositions, relative clauses, and track progress when the user consents. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persistent grammar DNA tracking and profile handoffs may expose learner-progress information if enabled without clear consent. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm where learner progress is stored, which Xiaozhi learning skills can receive handoffs, and how reminders or long-term profiling can be disabled before enabling tracking. <br>
