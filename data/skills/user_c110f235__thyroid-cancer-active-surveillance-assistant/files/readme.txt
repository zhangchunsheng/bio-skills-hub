# 甲状腺癌主动监测评估助手
# Thyroid Cancer Active Surveillance Assistant

---

## 项目简介 / About This Project

**中文：**
甲状腺癌主动监测（甲友吧），是微信公众号「甲友吧」博主 Rex 为甲状腺结节患者打造的主动监测科普参考与指南知识问答工具。

本项目整合多部国内外权威指南与超声分类标准，为患者提供基于循证医学的主动监测决策参考，帮助患者科学理解甲状腺结节/微小癌的随访、观察与管理路径。

**English:**
Thyroid Cancer Active Surveillance Assistant is an educational tool developed by Rex, creator of the WeChat public account "甲友吧" (Thyroid Friends Club). It provides evidence-based information and guideline-based Q&A on active surveillance for patients with thyroid nodules.

The tool synthesizes multiple authoritative clinical guidelines and ultrasound classification standards to help patients understand the follow-up, observation, and management pathways for thyroid nodules and microcarcinomas.

---

## 核心特点 / Key Features

**中文：**
- **多指南整合**：参考并整合多部权威指南
  - ATA 2025
  - NCCN 2025
  - KTA 2025
  - CACA 2025
  - KSThR 2024
  - JAES 2024
  - CMA 2023
- **标准化评估**：采用主流超声分类标准
  - C-TIRADS 2023
  - ACR TI-RADS
- **场景化问答**：覆盖患者最关心的核心问题
  - 主动监测的适用条件与流程
  - 甲状腺结节随访/微小癌观察方案
  - 穿刺指征与评估标准
  - TIRADS 分级解读
  - 甲状腺癌“不手术”的循证依据

**English:**
- **Multi-Guideline Integration**: Synthesizes recommendations from leading international and domestic guidelines:
  - ATA 2025
  - NCCN 2025
  - KTA 2025
  - CACA 2025
  - KSThR 2024
  - JAES 2024
  - CMA 2023
- **Standardized Assessment**: Uses widely accepted ultrasound classification systems:
  - C-TIRADS 2023
  - ACR TI-RADS
- **Patient-Centered Q&A**: Addresses key concerns of patients:
  - Eligibility and process for active surveillance
  - Follow-up and observation protocols for thyroid nodules/microcarcinomas
  - Indications and criteria for fine-needle aspiration (FNA)
  - Interpretation of TIRADS classification
  - Evidence-based rationale for non-surgical management of thyroid cancer
