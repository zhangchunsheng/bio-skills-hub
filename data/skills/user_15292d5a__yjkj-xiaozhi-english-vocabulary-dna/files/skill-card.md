## Description: <br>
Helps students build an English vocabulary review history and opt into spaced IM reminders, lesson preheating, and retention reports. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students and learning assistants use this skill to store English vocabulary, schedule consent-based spaced repetition reminders, prepare upcoming lesson vocabulary, and review vocabulary health over time. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can maintain vocabulary study history and recurring IM reminders. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm what vocabulary context is stored, which messaging channel is used, how reminders can be paused, and how stored vocabulary history can be deleted before enabling it. <br>
