# Techniques: Practical Nutritarian Tools

## The 6-Week Jumpstart

1. **Day 1-3**: Withdrawal phase. May experience headaches, fatigue, cravings. This is toxic hunger — it passes.
2. **Day 4-7**: Energy returns. Cravings diminish. Taste buds begin to shift.
3. **Week 2-3**: Weight loss accelerates. Cravings minimal. Natural foods taste better.
4. **Week 4-6**: New normal established. Results visible. Health markers improving.

## Building the Nutritarian Plate

Each meal should follow this structure:
- **Large salad** (at least 6 cups of greens) — the foundation
- **Cooked vegetables** — grilled, steamed, or stir-fried
- **Beans or legumes** — lentil soup, bean chili, chickpea curry
- **Mushrooms** — any variety, cooked
- **Berries for dessert** — fresh or frozen, no added sugar
- **Seeds** — flax, chia, hemp, sunflower — sprinkled on salads or blended

## Grocery Priority List

Always stock:
- Dark leafy greens (kale, arugula, romaine, collards)
- All varieties of mushrooms
- All varieties of beans and lentils (canned or dried)
- Onions, garlic, leeks
- Fresh or frozen berries
- Seeds: flax, chia, hemp, sunflower, sesame, pumpkin
- Cruciferous vegetables: broccoli, cabbage, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts
- Tomatoes, carrots, peppers, zucchini (any vegetables)

## Handling Cravings

When a craving hits:
1. Wait 15 minutes — drink water. Most cravings pass.
2. Eat a piece of fruit — the natural sugar satisfies the urge without feeding addiction.
3. Recognize it as toxic hunger — not true need.
4. Eat a large salad — nutrient density eliminates cravings at the cellular level.
