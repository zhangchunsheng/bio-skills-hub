## Description: <br>
Dr. Joel Fuhrman's Eat to Live helps an agent provide educational Nutritarian diet guidance for nutrient density, weight loss, meal planning, habit change, and disease-prevention discussions. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heardlyapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/heardlyapp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this agent skill to receive educational, book-grounded nutrition guidance based on Dr. Joel Fuhrman's Nutritarian framework. It supports nutrient-density education, rapid but sustainable weight-loss planning, grocery and meal planning, food-addiction habit support, and medical-adjacent diet discussions that should be reviewed with a clinician. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may provide confident nutrition guidance for serious health conditions, including disease-reversal claims. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as educational guidance only and direct users to consult a licensed clinician before major dietary changes. <br>
Risk: Users may be taking medications, considering procedures, or facing urgent symptoms that diet guidance alone cannot safely address. <br>
Mitigation: Tell users not to change medications, defer procedures, delay urgent care, or replace medical advice based on the skill's output. <br>
