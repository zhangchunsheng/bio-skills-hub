# Chapter 14: Histopathology (第十四章 组织病理学检查认可要求与迎检思路)

## Core Idea
Histopathology is a predominantly qualitative discipline (定性检测) built on manual operation and subjective interpretation, so quality assurance/control activities are themselves achieved through subjective judgement — making QA/QC harder here than in instrument-driven disciplines (p.515). Its second peculiarity: after a routine result is released, the pre-examination/examination/post-examination cycle can **re-enter** for special stains, IHC and molecular pathology, which compounds internal-QC complexity (p.515).

## Discipline-Specific Requirements

### 总体要求 (General Requirements) [4.1–4.3]
- **4.2.1 信息管理**: every histopathology staff member must sign a patient-privacy/confidentiality agreement; the lab needs concrete day-to-day privacy measures covering **both computerised and non-computerised** information (p.515–516).
- **4.2.2 信息发布**: pathology reports and related disclosures (e.g., informed-consent forms) may be received **only by the patient in person or a person the patient has authorised**, barring specific legal exceptions; information obtained from complainants/regulators stays confidential and its source is not disclosed to the patient unless the provider agrees (p.516).
- **4.3 患者相关的要求 a)–c)**: a channel for patients/users to supply information; public disclosure of test items, methods, method limitations, fees and report turnaround time; periodic review of whether examinations offered remain clinically appropriate (p.516).
- **4.3 d)–i)**: disclose harm-causing events; treat patients/samples/residual material with respect; obtain informed consent when needed; preserve sample/record continuity on lab closure or merger; provide information on request; ensure non-discriminatory access (p.517). **Retention**: pathology specimens — explicitly including paraffin-embedded blocks and nucleic-acid extracts — must be kept complete for "规定的期限" (a prescribed period); no numeric year figure is printed on this page, and local law/regulation governs the actual duration (p.517).

### 结构和管理要求 (Structure & Management) [5.1–5.6]
- **5.3.3 咨询活动 a)–d)**: advise on required sample type and the clinical indications/limitations of the examination method and request frequency; supply professional judgement for result interpretation; promote effective use of lab examinations; advise on scientific/administrative matters (e.g., samples failing acceptance criteria) (p.517–518). Evidence: consultation-system records and 临床病理讨论 (CPC) records; on-site interviews of clinical staff about consultation effectiveness and the two-way clinician-communication channel for new services.
- **5.5 目标和方针**: objectives must be measurable and consistent with policy (b); management must preserve system integrity through change (c); the lab must set **质量指标** for pre-examination/examination/post-examination critical steps and monitor performance (d, cross-ref 8.8.2) (p.518). Standard's own 注 lists indicator types: unacceptable-sample count, registration/reception errors, corrected-report count, TAT-completion rate.
- **★ Illustrative quality-objective exemplars (【标准解读】, explicitly NOT mandated CNAS thresholds)**: 标本合格率 (specimen acceptability) **>95%**; 诊断符合率 (diagnostic concordance) **>98%**; 开展新项目 (new tests/services) **>2 项/年** (p.518).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The 5+1 discipline-specific risk framework** (opening essay, p.515): (其一) personnel operation/interpretation dominate the result across grossing–embedding–sectioning–diagnosis; (其二) personnel–equipment interaction (e.g., microtome) where final section quality is judged subjectively; (其三) environment/facility risk differs by area (取材室 vs 常规技术室 — biohazard vs chemical-hazard emphasis); (其四) standardised specimen fixation and pre-treatment of resected specimens is the critical, most-easily-lost-control link; (其五) routine diagnosis, intraoperative frozen-section diagnosis and targeted molecular testing are high-attention discipline-specific links; (最后) clinician/user communication reduces risk system-wide. When to use: scoping a histopathology-specific risk assessment. How: walk each of the six points against the lab's own SOPs and staffing before an on-site visit.

## Key Concepts
- **Qualitative testing (定性检测)** — the discipline's defining trait; drives subjective, personnel-centred QA/QC [opening essay, p.515].
- **Repeating examination cycle** — routine diagnosis can re-trigger the pre-exam/exam/post-exam cycle for special stains, IHC or molecular testing [p.515].
- **Consultation/advisory activity (咨询活动)** — advice on sample type, method limitations, result interpretation and effective utilisation; evidenced via CPC records [5.3.3, p.517–518].
- **Quality indicator (质量指标)** — measurable metric tied to pre/exam/post-exam critical steps; must connect to the lab's stated objectives [5.5, p.518].
- **IQC (室内质量控制)** — internal QC, illustrated with an IHC/molecular marker example (HER2) [7.3.7.2, p.542].
- **EQA/PT (室间质量评价/能力验证)** — external quality assessment/proficiency testing; provider competence referenced to GB/T 27043/ISO-IEC 17043 [7.3.7.3, p.543].
- **MU (测量不确定度)** — measurement uncertainty, referenced to ISO/TS 20914 [7.3.4–7.3.5, p.539–540].
- **HIS/LIS interface** — hospital/laboratory information-system integration discussed under data/information management [7.6.3–7.6.4, p.552].
- **TAT (turnaround time)** — cited at clause 7.8 without a specific numeric target recoverable [p.554].

## Mental Models
- **Use the qualitative-testing lens** whenever a finding centres on personnel competence rather than an instrument printout — grossing, embedding, sectioning and interpretation are all judgement calls, not measurements.
- **Treat a re-triggered examination (routine → special stain/IHC/molecular) as a new entry into the 7.3 cycle**, not a post-hoc addendum, when assessing IQC completeness.
- **Read the >95%/>98%/>2-per-year figures as illustrative benchmarks**, not fixed pass/fail bars — the standard's own wording ("诸如…等") frames them as examples a lab may adapt.
- **When a clause range shows only a skeleton (clause numbers + cited standards, no narrative)**, treat that as a signal to verify the physical book or local SOP directly rather than infer content from an adjacent discipline chapter.

## Anti-patterns
- **Treating >95% specimen-acceptability / >98% diagnostic-concordance / >2-new-items-per-year as CNAS-mandated pass marks**: the source explicitly frames these as worked examples ("诸如…等"), not required thresholds — a lab may set different, self-justified figures under 5.5.
- **Allowing a companion, family member, or unauthenticated self-service kiosk request to release a report**: 4.2.2 restricts release to the patient in person or an authorised person barring statutory exceptions.
- **Assuming a fixed retention year-count for paraffin blocks/nucleic-acid extracts**: the text says only "规定的期限" (a prescribed period) with no number printed at this location — citing an invented figure would misstate the standard.
- **Asserting "PT/EQA participation ≥2×/year" from p.544**: the digits 1–2 are confirmed but the period unit is illegible; stating "year" would be an invented unit.
- **Filling the 6.1–6.5 gap (personnel/facilities/equipment/calibration) with figures borrowed from the cytopathology chapter**: histopathology-specific personnel ratios, fixation parameters and equipment rules were not recoverable in this chapter's material and must not be assumed identical to Ch 15's cytopathology numbers.

## Worked Example
*(No 案例 boxes appear in the source material for this chapter's readable range; the following is a constructed illustration, not a book case.)* An assessor tests the self-service pathology-report printing kiosk (自助打印系统) using only a specimen accession number, without any patient-identity or authorisation check. The kiosk dispenses the report. **Finding**: this breaches 4.2.2 (release limited to the patient in person or an authorised person) and undercuts 4.3's respect-for-patient-rights intent. **Root cause**: the kiosk's function settings were never reviewed against the confidentiality procedure referenced in 4.2.1. **Corrective action**: add an identity/authorisation-verification step to the kiosk workflow, retrain registration staff, and audit a sample of prior kiosk releases for undisclosed leakage.

## Key Takeaways
- Histopathology QA/QC is personnel-centred by design: grossing, embedding, sectioning and interpretation are all subjective steps, so competence — not instrument output — is the primary risk surface (p.515).
- The examination cycle can loop back (routine → special stain/IHC/molecular), so IQC planning must anticipate re-entry, not just first-pass release (p.515).
- Confidentiality/patient-rights clauses (4.2–4.3) require both a signed staff agreement and concrete, auditable release-channel controls such as kiosk-access verification.
- The >95%/>98%/>2-per-year figures are illustrative benchmarks the interpretation offers, not mandated CNAS thresholds (p.518).
- For clauses 6.1–6.5 and much of 7.2–8.9, this chapter's source material yields only a clause-number skeleton, cited external/national standards, and a handful of Latin acronyms (POCT, SOP, IQC, EQA, PT, MU, TAT, LIS/HIS, LOINC/NPU/SNOMED CT/SI) — specific numeric criteria for fixation, retention, staffing and frozen-section performance must be verified directly against the physical standard or local SOPs.
