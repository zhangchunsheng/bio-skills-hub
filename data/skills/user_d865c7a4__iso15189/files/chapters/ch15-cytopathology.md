# Chapter 15: Cytopathology (第十五章 细胞病理学检查认可要求与迎检思路)

## Core Idea
Cytopathology, like histopathology, is a qualitative discipline, but ISO 15189:2022 (CNAS-CL02, in force **December 2023**) reorganises its application document around risk/resource/process management with a results-based rather than prescriptive-method emphasis (p.557). This chapter's best-verified content is personnel workload/competence and facility/equipment requirements (pp.557–570); the pre-examination, examination and management-system back half (pp.571–600) survives mostly as a clause skeleton with cited external standards.

## Discipline-Specific Requirements

### 人员 (Personnel) [6.2]
- **Gynaecological (妇科) screening workload cap**: each screener may read **no more than 100 slides per working day**, to safeguard report quality; non-gynaecological cytology staffing is instead increased "as appropriate" per the number of test items undertaken, with no numeric cap printed; if AI-assisted diagnosis is used for screening, staff numbers **may be reduced appropriately** [6.2.1, p.561].
- **Qualifications**: general staff need a clinical-medicine/pathology degree, a physician licence and/or pathology professional-title certificate, post-specific training/assessment with authorisation, and **normal colour vision** [6.2.2, p.562]. **Technical manager (技术负责人)**: ≥**3 years** in cytopathology work [6.2.2, p.562]. **Authorised signatory (授权签字人)**: **intermediate professional title or above** plus ≥**3 years** in the accredited field [6.2.2, p.562]. Physicians independently issuing cytopathology reports need a pathologist qualification certificate, practice licence, professional-title certificate and verified relevant work years [6.2.2, p.562].
- **Competence assessment**: staff doing morphological identification get **at least 2 assessments within the first 6 months**; **annual** assessment applies to all staff thereafter; staff away from post **≥6 months** must be retrained and reassessed, as must anyone changing jobs in a way that could affect result quality [6.2.2–6.2.3, p.562–563].
- **Authorisation (6.2.3)**: individual sign-off required for method selection/validation/verification, result review/release/reporting, and LIS access (especially patient-data entry/modification) [p.563].
- **Records/audit**: assessors **sample 10% of personnel files** [6.2.5, p.563]; a separate interview check samples **1 person from each of 4 categories** (staff, students, workers, engineers) for confidentiality-awareness [4.2.1, p.557].

### 设施与环境 (Facilities & Environment) [6.3]
- **Functional zones (6.3.1)**: reception, storage, grossing (取材), slide preparation, diagnosis and archive storage; a lab performing **fine-needle aspiration (细针穿刺, FNA) must have a dedicated puncture examination room**; staff/goods/specimen flows must satisfy biosafety and nosocomial-infection-control requirements (referencing `ISO 15190`) [p.564].
- **Facility control (6.3.2)**: documented temperature/humidity control with records and out-of-control handling; **UPS and/or dual power supply** for critical continuously-monitored equipment, with periodic charge/discharge function-check records if a UPS is installed; **when T/RH is logged manually, record at least twice** (per the stated cycle); a cold-chain logging system and periodic thermometer/hygrometer calibration are recommended [p.564].
- **Storage/staff/collection facilities (6.3.3–6.3.5)**: target temperature + permitted range set/recorded for reagent/sample storage, plus a **dedicated hazardous-chemical storage cabinet**; sufficient toilets and recommended changing/drinking-water facilities; specimen-collection area needs a **first-aid kit, periodically checked and within expiry**, disabled access, washing facilities, and accommodation for a guardian/interpreter (`ISO 20658` referenced) [p.565].

### 设备与试剂 (Equipment & Reagents) [6.4–6.6]
- **Equipment inventory (6.4.2)**: biosafety cabinet, liquid-based cytology (LBC) processor, slide scanner, stainer, microscope, centrifuge — each with a unique equipment identifier [p.566].
- **Acceptance procedure (6.4.3)**: installation acceptance, calibration, performance verification, comparison testing, training/authorisation, **pathology (PACS) data-consistency verification**, and instrument-SOP writing [p.566].
- **Usage/on-site check (6.4.4)**: differentiated user-privilege levels prevent accidental parameter changes; assessors typically **sample 1 instrument and 2 staff members** to observe operating competence [p.566–567].
- **Maintenance/repair (6.4.5)**: after a fault that may affect methodological performance, choose from a defined menu — **calibration verification (recalibrate if needed) / QC-material testing / comparison with another instrument or method / re-testing previously examined samples**; a documented single-instrument-failure risk-assessment/contingency procedure is required, and faulty equipment must be clearly quarantine-labelled until verified [p.567].
- **Equipment records (6.4.7)** a)–k) cover manufacturer/supplier + unique ID, receipt/acceptance/service-entry dates, conformity evidence, location, new/second-hand/refurbished condition, PM plan, maintenance provider, damage/repair history, calibration/verification records (incl. date, time, result), and status. **Retention: the equipment's service life or longer, per procedure not less than 2 years**, electronic records permitted (6.4.7 via 8.4.3) [p.568].
- **Calibration & traceability (6.5)**: qualitative/non-metrologically-traceable examples explicitly include **flow-cytometry marker staining and tumour HER2 immunohistochemical staining**, gene testing, ESR, and antibiotic-susceptibility assessment [6.5.1, p.568]. Centrifuge, pipettes, measuring cylinders and thermo-hygrometers need verification (检定) records/reports; the **centrifugal-force calibration report must at minimum cover the routinely used g-force range** and be document-controlled [6.5.2, p.569]. Because cytopathology is qualitative, quantitative metrological traceability does not apply; traceability for qualitative methods (including special stains and IHC) is instead shown via **comparison against known-positive specimens** (positive/negative agreement or intensity agreement), or by **PT/EQA participation with passing results**, **inter-laboratory comparison within the specified allowable error**, or reliance on standards/guidelines/expert consensus [6.5.3, p.569–570]. Gene testing must be traceable to a gene reference sequence [6.5.3 d), p.570].
- **Reagents/consumables (6.6.1–6.6.3)**: documented process for selection/procurement/receipt/storage/acceptance/inventory, including in-house-prepared reagents; on delivery, check **name, quantity, specification, lot number, manufacture date, outer packaging, transport/storage conditions**, with **on-the-spot rejection if non-compliant** [6.6.2, p.570]. Acceptance testing is triggered by **each new reagent/kit formulation, new lot number, or new shipment number**, verified via parallel testing against specimens with previously known results [6.6.3, p.570].

### 质量保证 / 管理体系要求 (Quality Assurance / Management System) [5.5–5.6, 8.x]

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Workload-based staffing framework** [6.2.1, p.561]: a 100-slide/person/working-day ceiling for gynaecological cytology, scaled staffing for non-gynaecological items, and headcount reduction where AI-assisted screening is used. When to use: sizing or auditing a cytopathology screening bench. How: compare monthly **and** daily workload logs against the 100-slide cap per screener, adjusted for test-item breadth and AI-tool deployment.
- **Post-fault requalification menu** [6.4.5, p.567]: calibration verification (recalibrate if needed) / QC-material testing / comparison with another instrument or method / re-testing previously examined samples. When to use: after any equipment fault that may affect methodological performance. How: select the appropriate check(s), document the outcome, and quarantine-label the instrument until verified.
- **Qualitative-traceability substitution framework** [6.5.3, p.569–570]: where quantitative metrological traceability is impossible — the default for cytopathology — substitute known-positive-specimen comparison, PT/EQA participation with passing results, inter-laboratory comparison within an allowable error, or reliance on standards/guidelines/expert consensus. When to use: any qualitative stain or marker method (e.g., HER2 IHC). How: document at least one of the four alternative-evidence types per test system.
- **Risk-management cycle** [5.6, p.560–561]: identify → assess → control → evaluate effectiveness, applied jointly to medical-quality/safety and biosafety risk, graded by severity × frequency. When to use: building or maintaining the lab's risk register. How: score each identified risk on both axes, document controls, and re-evaluate effectiveness on a defined cycle.

## Key Concepts
- **Optimal-slide rate (优片率)** — a recommended cytopathology-specific quality indicator; no numeric target printed [5.5, p.560].
- **Cytology–histology diagnostic concordance rate (细胞学与组织学诊断的符合率)** — recommended indicator comparing cytology calls against subsequent histology; no numeric target printed [5.5, p.560].
- **Gynaecological screening cap (妇科细胞学阅片工作量上限)** — ≤100 slides/person/working day, to safeguard report quality [6.2.1, p.561].
- **Authorised signatory (授权签字人)** — intermediate professional title or above, plus ≥3 years' experience in the accredited field [6.2.2, p.562].
- **Post-fault requalification menu** — calibration verification / QC-material testing / instrument-or-method comparison / re-testing prior samples, chosen after an equipment fault [6.4.5, p.567].
- **Qualitative traceability substitute** — known-positive-specimen comparison or PT/EQA/inter-lab comparison used in place of quantitative metrological traceability [6.5.3, p.569].
- **PACS (病理图像归档和通信系统)** — pathology picture archiving/communication system; data consistency verified at equipment acceptance and cross-referenced at specimen receipt [6.4.3, 7.2.6.1, pp.566, 578].
- **IQC/EQA (室内质量控制/室间质量评价)** — internal vs. external quality control; EQA/PT-provider competence referenced to GB/T 27043/ISO-IEC 17043 [7.3.7.2–7.3.7.3, pp.584–586].
- **FNA (细针穿刺)** — fine-needle aspiration; a lab performing FNA must have a dedicated puncture examination room [6.3.1, p.564].

## Mental Models
- **Use the "screener-workload cap as quality lever" model**: the 100-slide/day gynaecological-cytology ceiling exists specifically "to safeguard report quality," so treat any breach as a direct quality-risk finding, not merely a staffing/HR issue.
- **Use the "qualitative discipline, substitute traceability" model** whenever equipment/method traceability is questioned: since cytopathology is explicitly qualitative, the correct question is which of the four substitute-evidence types (known-positive comparison / PT-EQA / inter-lab comparison / consensus standard) is documented — not "where is the calibration curve?"
- **Use "unattributed numeric flags as investigation triggers, not conclusions"** for the two OCR-uncertain thresholds in this chapter (an unattributed "<5%" near 7.3.3, and a recurring "≥80%"-style figure near 7.3.7.3–7.3.7.4): treat them as pointers to re-check the exact source page and local procedure, not as citable pass/fail criteria.
- **Use "functional-zone completeness" as the facility-walkthrough checklist**: reception → storage → grossing → slide preparation → diagnosis → archive, plus a dedicated puncture room only if FNA is performed [6.3.1].

## Anti-patterns
- **Applying the 100-slide/day cap to non-gynaecological cytology**: the notes state only that non-gyn staffing should increase "as appropriate," with no numeric ceiling — treating 100 as a universal cap misapplies a gynaecological-cytology-specific figure.
- **Citing the "<5%" (p.581) or the recurring "≥80%"-style figure (pp.586–587) as confirmed pass/fail thresholds**: both were extracted with reasonable numeral confidence, but their Chinese subject/unit could not be read; presenting either as a settled criterion in an assessment would cite an unverified figure.
- **Treating the named cytopathology quality indicators (优片率, 细胞学与组织学诊断的符合率, etc.) as CNAS-mandated numeric targets**: the source records these as a named indicator list with no printed target values — labs must set and justify their own under 5.5.
- **Skipping the post-fault requalification menu in favour of one ad hoc QC check**: 6.4.5 defines four possible re-qualification routes plus a documented risk-assessment/contingency procedure per instrument.
- **Assuming equipment records need only the minimum 2 years**: 6.4.7/8.4.3 requires retention for the equipment's service life or longer, with 2 years stated as a floor, not a target.

## Worked Example
*(No 案例 boxes appear anywhere in the readable material for this chapter; the following is a constructed illustration, not a book case.)* During an on-site assessment, the assessor pulls both monthly and daily workload logs for the gynaecological-cytology screening bench and finds one screener averaging 118 slides on 6 of the preceding 20 working days, with no AI-assisted pre-screening in use. Cross-checking the 10% personnel-file sample shows that screener's last competence assessment was 14 months earlier, exceeding the annual cycle. **Finding**: two linked nonconformities — workload-cap exceedance under 6.2.1 and an overdue competence assessment under 6.2.2. **Root cause**: a temporary staffing shortfall was not compensated by hiring, AI-assisted triage, or caseload reduction. **Corrective action**: redistribute caseload, complete the overdue assessment before the screener resumes unsupervised reporting, and add a workload-vs-cap monitoring alert.

## Key Takeaways
- Personnel and equipment/facility numeric requirements (6.2–6.5) are this chapter's best-verified content: the 100-slide/day gynaecological-cytology cap, the ≥3-year technical-manager/authorised-signatory experience floor, the ≥2-assessments-in-6-months plus annual competence-assessment cycle, and the ≥2-year equipment-record retention floor are all directly citable.
- Cytopathology is explicitly qualitative, so traceability and IQC are demonstrated through comparison/consensus mechanisms (known-positive specimens, PT/EQA, inter-lab comparison) rather than calibration curves.
- Five cytopathology-specific quality indicators are named (不合格标本率、优片率、细胞学与组织学诊断符合率、报告及时率、投诉处理率) but none carries a printed numeric target — labs must define and justify their own.
- Two numeric thresholds recur in the examination clauses (an unattributed "<5%" near 7.3.3, and a recurring "≥80%"-style figure near 7.3.7.3–7.3.7.4) whose subject could not be verified — treat them as leads to re-check against the physical standard, not settled criteria.
- Printed pages 594 and 599 are missing from the source scan entirely (containing clauses 7.7.1 and 8.6/8.8.1) — a source-document gap, not a transcription failure.
- The back half of the chapter (7.2 pre-examination through 8.9 management review) survives only as a clause skeleton plus cited external standards (ISO 20658/20166/20184/20186 series, ISO/TS 20914, ISO 17511, ISO 22367, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, CLSI GP36-A, GB/T 27043/ISO-IEC 17043) — narrative detail on adequacy criteria and TBS reporting was not recoverable.
