# Chapter 19: Applying for Accreditation and Preparing for Assessment (医学实验室认可申请与迎检准备)

## Core Idea
Accreditation is a gatekept, evidence-driven cycle, not a paperwork submission: CNAS (中国合格评定国家认可委员会) will only accept an application once a lab can *prove* its CNAS-CL02-based (医学实验室质量和能力认可准则, 等同采用ISO 15189:2022) management system has actually operated and produced clinical results, and will only grant accreditation once on-site assessors have verified — through witnessed testing, record review, and interviews — that every applied-for item is real, competently performed, and backed by satisfactory external quality checks. The chapter is organized around the document system that governs the rules, the end-to-end process, the acceptance gate, the on-site mechanics, and the paperwork that must accompany the application.

## Process / Form Walkthrough

### CNAS document system (19.1)
Documents form a tier: (1) **Rules (认可规则)** — CNAS-RL01《实验室认可规则》governs the accreditation system itself; CNAS-RL02《能力验证规则》governs PT/EQA obligations. (2) **Criteria (认可准则)** — CNAS-CL01 (general testing/calibration labs, ≈ISO/IEC 17025) vs. **CNAS-CL02** (医学实验室质量和能力认可准则, ≈ISO 15189:2022) — the criteria medical labs are actually assessed against. (3) **Application Notes (应用说明, CNAS-CL02-Axxx series)** — one per medical discipline (临床血液体液、临床化学、临床免疫、临床微生物、分子诊断、输血医学、POCT, etc.), clarifying how CL02 clauses apply to that specialty's techniques. (4) **Guidance (指南, CNAS-GL series)** — non-mandatory practical guidance (method validation/verification, uncertainty, reference intervals). Labs must always obtain the *current valid edition* from CNAS's official site before preparing an application — using a superseded edition is flagged as a recurring, avoidable failure.

### Accreditation process, stage by stage (19.2)
1. **Application submission (提出申请)** — lab completes the application form + all annexes/attachments and submits to CNAS's Secretariat (paper or online system).
2. **Acceptance review (受理)** — CNAS checks completeness and eligibility; issues 受理/不受理 with reasons. Only accepted applications proceed.
3. **Contract & scheduling** — CNAS and the lab confirm the assessment agreement; CNAS forms the assessment team (组长 + specialty assessors matched to declared disciplines + technical experts as needed) and sets the on-site date.
4. **Document review (文件评审)** — team reviews the quality manual/procedures against CNAS-CL02 before the visit; issues raised must be addressed by/at the on-site stage.
5. **On-site assessment (现场评审)** — opening meeting → system implementation review, record/interview checks, witnessed testing → closing meeting presenting findings.
6. **Nonconformity correction (不符合项整改)** — root-cause analysis, corrective action, and closure evidence submitted within the severity-linked deadline (see Reference Tables).
7. **Assessment report & technical review** — team submits the report; CNAS's internal review verifies NC closure evidence.
8. **Accreditation decision (认可评定)** — CNAS's approving body makes the formal decision.
9. **Certificate issuance (颁发证书)** — certificate states the accredited scope and validity period.
10. **Post-accreditation surveillance (获证后监督)** — periodic surveillance assessments during validity.
11. **Reassessment (复评审)** — renewal cycle before expiry, run essentially as a fresh full-process application.

### Acceptance requirements (19.3)
Before CNAS will even accept an application, the lab must show: legal-entity status (or authorized internal unit of one) able to bear legal responsibility; a CNAS-CL02-conformant management system that has been *effectively operating* for a minimum period with objective records (not merely documented on paper); at least one completed internal audit and one management review spanning the whole system and the applied scope; every applied item already in *routine clinical use* with accumulated results; satisfactory PT/EQA participation for applied items (or a documented valid alternative where no scheme exists); already-appointed and qualified key roles (技术负责人、质量负责人、授权签字人); and no active suspension or unresolved impartiality-affecting disputes. Administratively, the application must also match the checklist in 19.7 and fees must be paid per CNAS's schedule.

### On-site assessment mechanics (19.4)
Team size/composition and assessment duration scale with the number of applied disciplines, sites, and staff, and with assessment type (initial/extension/reassessment/surveillance). The visit covers documents, records, interviews, and *witnessed* testing/operational skill checks, sometimes using blind or retained samples. Findings are classified **severe/major nonconformity (严重不符合项)** vs. **general/minor nonconformity (一般不符合项)**: major findings signal a systemic breakdown or risk to result validity and typically require a follow-up on-site verification (跟踪检查) before accreditation proceeds; minor findings can usually be closed via corrective-action evidence review alone. The closing meeting records the team's overall recommendation, acknowledged/signed by the lab's representative.

### Application-form and annex/attachment filling requirements (19.5-19.7)
The application form's identity section must exactly match the lab's registered legal name and practicing-license address(es); every site must be listed with role; the applied criteria (CNAS-CL02 + relevant Axxx notes) must be explicitly ticked; and the form closes with signed declarations of accuracy, compliance, impartiality, and confidentiality. Section 19.6 enumerates the required annexes (授权签字人一览表, 检验能力范围表, 自查表, PT/比对汇总表, 人员一览表, 项目清单— detailed in Ch.20). Section 19.7 lists the supporting documents submitted alongside: legal-status/practicing-license proof, quality manual + procedure list, most-recent internal audit and management review reports, method validation/verification reports for applied items, staff qualification certificates (especially signatories), PT/EQA result certificates, and fee payment proof.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **CNAS document tier (规则→准则→应用说明→指南)**: Rules (RL) set the accreditation system's own procedures; Criteria (CL01/CL02) set what's assessed; Application Notes (CL02-Axxx) localize criteria to a discipline; Guidance (GL) is optional interpretive support. Use when determining which document governs a specific clause question — always trace up from the discipline-specific Axxx note to the parent CL02 clause.
- **Severity-linked nonconformity closure**: 严重不符合项 → follow-up on-site verification generally required; 一般不符合项 → evidence-based remote closure generally sufficient. Use this to decide whether a corrective action needs a repeat visit before the accreditation decision can be made.
- **Scope-to-annex traceability chain**: 附表6 (full menu) ⊇ 附表2 (applied scope) ↔ 附表4 (PT coverage, 1:1) ↔ 附表3 (clause self-check evidence) ↔ 附表1 (signatory authority) ⊆ 附表5 (full roster). Use when auditing your own application package for internal consistency before submission.

## Key Concepts
- **CNAS-CL02 (医学实验室质量和能力认可准则)** — the accreditation criteria medical labs are assessed against, equivalent to ISO 15189:2022.
- **CNAS-CL02-Axxx (应用说明)** — discipline-specific application notes attached to CL02.
- **受理 (Acceptance)** — CNAS's completeness/eligibility screening before an assessment team is assigned.
- **现场评审 (On-site Assessment)** — the physical site visit: opening meeting, review, witnessed testing, closing meeting.
- **严重不符合项 / 一般不符合项 (Major/Minor Nonconformity)** — severity classes governing closure requirements.
- **跟踪检查 (Follow-up Verification)** — a repeat on-site check to close a major nonconformity.
- **授权签字人 (Authorized Signatory)** — staff authorized to sign reports within a personally-qualified scope.
- **能力验证/室间质量评价 (PT/EQA)** — required external result-comparison evidence per applied item.
- **监督评审 (Surveillance Assessment)** — periodic post-accreditation on-site checks.
- **复评审 (Reassessment)** — renewal-cycle full reapplication before certificate expiry.

## Mental Models
- Think of 受理 as a locked gate: incomplete eligibility evidence (system-operation history, completed audit/review cycle, PT results) never even reaches an assessor's calendar.
- Use the severity split (major vs. minor NC) whenever deciding *how much re-verification* a fix needs — major always assumes the system itself may still be broken until proven otherwise on-site.
- Think of the annex set (附表1-6) as one interlocking dataset, not six separate forms — any inconsistency between them (e.g., an item in 附表2 missing from 附表4) is itself an audit trail assessors will pull on.

## Anti-patterns
- **Applying before the system has a real operating track record**: a management system that exists only on paper, without completed internal-audit/management-review cycles and accumulated routine results, fails at the 受理 gate.
- **Submitting an outdated CNAS document edition** as the basis for self-assessment: since CL01/CL02/Axxx/GL documents are periodically revised, referencing a superseded edition invalidates the self-check and application-note mapping.
- **Applying for items without satisfactory PT/EQA coverage or a documented alternative**: unsupported or unsatisfactory-without-correction entries in the PT summary are treated as serious grounds for rejecting that scope item.
- **Letting a signatory's authorized scope exceed personal qualification/experience**: even if the lab overall is competent, an individual signatory's authority must not outrun their own demonstrated expertise.
- **Inconsistent annexes**: an item appearing in 附表2 (applied scope) but missing from 附表4 (PT coverage), 附表6 (full menu), or with a mismatched name/method, undermines the whole package's credibility during on-site review.

## Key Takeaways
- Accreditation is gated twice: once at 受理 (eligibility/completeness) and again at 现场评审 (verified competence), not a single paperwork checkpoint.
- The CNAS document tier (RL → CL → Axxx → GL) determines which document answers which question — always resolve discipline-specific questions via the matching Axxx note under CL02.
- Nonconformity severity (major vs. minor) is the single variable that determines whether a repeat on-site visit is needed before accreditation is granted.
- The six application annexes form one cross-checked dataset; internal inconsistency across them is itself a red flag to assessors.
- Accreditation is cyclical (surveillance + reassessment), not a one-time grant.
