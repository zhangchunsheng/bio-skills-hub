# Theme Specifications

Use the selected section together with `theme_registry.md` and `typography_layout.md`.

## Theme 1 · Biomedical clay cutaway

Use one continuous matte polymer-clay cellular cutaway: targeted LNP circulation, receptor binding, endocytosis, endosomal escape, RNA release and CAR expression. Cream, terracotta, teal, mustard, burgundy and deep blue. Preserve a natural cream quiet field on the left. No separate cards, toy characters, glossy plastic or generated text.

## Theme 2 · Nature science concept

Use one monumental translucent molecular subject with a precise antibody-to-CD8 interaction and a luminous RNA payload. Midnight blue, ice cyan, cold white and coral. Keep the upper-left naturally dark and low-detail for typography. No mechanism panels or neon sci-fi.

## Theme 3 · Businessweek metaphor

Use one readable commercial metaphor for valuation/evidence mismatch: oversized gold targeted LNP object versus tiny clinical evidence objects. Acid yellow, black, metallic gold and clinical red. Regenerate with a clean left quiet field when needed. No money symbols, stock charts, corporate towers or readable text.

## Theme 4 · Monocle industry observation

Use a calm axonometric ecosystem of VHH design, LNP formulation, delivery, T-cell engineering, quality control and patient-care spaces. Warm ivory, forest green, brick red, grey blue and mustard. Keep the left architectural field quiet. No glossy lab or holographic UI.

## Theme 5 · Microscopic documentary

Use a credible microscopic binding event between antibody-decorated LNP and a folded CD8 membrane, with restrained fluorescence at the binding site. Graphite, deep sea blue, silver white and fluorescence green. Keep a dark defocused left region for type. No virus cliché or cartoon cells.

## Theme 6 · Swiss poster

Reduce the proposition to two to four geometric objects, disciplined black lines and a modular paper-white grid. Use black, cobalt blue and signal red. Preserve left negative space and asymmetric balance. No gradients, 3D cells or UI.

## Theme 7 · Science archive

Layer patent-style lipid/VHH drawings, microfluidic sketches, microscopy plate fragments and abstract red editorial marks on old paper. Charcoal, faded navy, oxide red and experiment green. Keep an unprinted paper field on the left. No readable patent numbers or modern HUD.

## Theme 8 · Pipeline map

Start from targeted delivery and branch into antibody targeting, lipid tropism, RNA payload and clinical evidence endpoints. Use transit-like curves, circular nodes, microscopy inserts and warm grey, navy, teal, burgundy and gold. Regenerate a left quiet field if the source is crowded. No company names or dense labels.

## Theme 9 · Clinical evidence bluebook

Center the therapy object and surround it with sparse cohort points, dose-response trajectories, repeat-dose windows, microscopy slices and evidence planes. Clinical white, navy, cool cyan, graphite and safety orange. Reserve a clean masthead field. No dashboard cards or fake numbers.

## Theme 10 · Cell mechanism atlas

Use one continuous publication-grade cellular cross-section showing targeting, internalization, endosomal escape, mRNA translation and CAR-T engagement. Deep navy, cyan membrane, burgundy vesicles, cold white and RNA gold. Keep a dark quiet publication masthead zone. No boxed panels or textbook cartoon styling.

## Theme 11 · Medical congress key visual

Use ordered particles and molecular fragments converging into a monumental therapeutic CAR-T subject; show RNA glow and an immune-reset horizon. Deep indigo, medical cyan, cold white and magenta. Regenerate a quiet left atmospheric field when needed. No stage, speaker, audience or explosion.

## Theme 12 · Molecular blueprint

Use a central targeted-LNP cutaway with lipid shell, ionizable core, PEG linker, VHH ligand and RNA payload, plus three magnification windows. Prussian blue, ice white, cyan linework and node yellow. Reserve a dark left blueprint field for light typography. No readable formulas, patent IDs or HUD.

## Theme 13 · Bioprocess engineering

Use a credible continuous cleanroom process from lipid/RNA inputs through LNP formation, antibody conjugation, TFF, filtration and final product. Clean white, titanium silver, cool blue, deep teal and amber. Keep a bright left cleanroom field. No impossible piping or fake equipment labels.
