## Description: <br>
Xiaozhi Physics Error Dna helps agents analyze recurring physics mistakes, classify root causes, and produce weak-point reports using a physics-specific error taxonomy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Students, tutors, and learning agents use this skill to turn physics mistakes into structured root-cause records, identify stubborn weak points, and generate targeted review summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Physics root-cause classifications or weak-point reports may be incomplete or incorrect if based on limited student history. <br>
Mitigation: Review diagnoses against the original problem, solution, and student reasoning before relying on recommendations. <br>
Risk: The workflow can create persistent student learning records and references anxiety-related signals. <br>
Mitigation: Create, share, or summarize student records only with user consent and limit cross-skill handoffs to the fields needed for the current task. <br>
