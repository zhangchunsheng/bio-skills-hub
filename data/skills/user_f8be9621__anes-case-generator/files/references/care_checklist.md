# Case Report Checklist: CARE + ACRE

> **Sources:**
> - CARE: Gagnier JJ, et al. The CARE guidelines: consensus-based clinical case reporting guideline development. *J Clin Epidemiol*. 2013;67(1):39-46.
> - ACRE: Shelton CL, et al. ACRE: the Anaesthesia Case REport guideline. *Anaesthesia Reports*. 2021.
>
> **Purpose:** 用于 Step 4 报告结构生成和 Step 5 完整性核验

> **Recommendation:** 推荐使用 **ACRE** 作为围术期病例报告的首选标准，CARE 作为通用补充参考。ACRE 在 CARE 基础上针对麻醉领域进行了结构优化，更贴合围术期病例报告的需求。

---

## Part 1: ACRE Checklist — 12 Items (Anaesthesia Case Report)

> Source: Shelton CL, et al. *Anaesthesia Reports*. 2021.
> ACRE 是专为麻醉/围术期病例报告设计的清单，在 CARE 基础上整合了麻醉特异性要求。

| # | ACRE Item | Requirement | CARE Mapping |
|:--|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **Title** | 关键词 + "case report" | CARE 1 |
| 2 | **Keywords** | 2-5 个关键词 | CARE 2 |
| 3 | **Abstract** | 结构化摘要（背景、病例摘要、结论） | CARE 3a-3d |
| 4 | **Introduction** | 为何本病例值得报告 | CARE 4 |
| 5 | **Focused patient and contextual information** | 去标识化人口学信息 + 手术/操作背景 | CARE 5a-5d |
| 6 | **Assessment, optimisation and consent** | 术前评估 + 优化策略 + 知情同意 + 识别的临床挑战及应对 | CARE 6, 8a-8d |
| 7 | **Interventions** | 详细描述术中管理（麻醉方案、药物、设备、非标准操作） | CARE 9a-9c |
| 8 | **Adverse/critical events** | 不良事件/危急事件（识别、管理、人为因素） | CARE 10d |
| 9 | **Follow-up and outcomes** | 术后随访和结局 | CARE 10a-10c |
| 10 | **Discussion** | 讨论（优势/局限 + 文献对比 + 实践建议和未来研究方向） | CARE 11a-11d |
| 11 | **Patient/carer perspective** | 患者/照护者视角 + 对未来知情同意有用的信息 | CARE 12 |
| 12 | **Informed consent** | 知情同意声明 | CARE 13 |

## Part 2: CARE Checklist — 30 Items (General Reference)

> Source: Gagnier JJ, et al. *J Clin Epidemiol*. 2013.
> CARE 是通用的病例报告指南，适用于所有医学专科。以下为完整 30 项清单，作为 ACRE 的补充参考。

| # | Topic | Requirement |
|:--|:---|:---|
| 1 | Title | Diagnosis/intervention + "case report" |
| 2 | Keywords | 2-5 keywords including "case report" |
| 3a | Abstract-Intro | What is unique? What does it add? |
| 3b | Abstract-Symptoms | Main symptoms & clinical findings |
| 3c | Abstract-Interventions | Main diagnoses, interventions, outcomes |
| 3d | Abstract-Conclusion | Main take-away lesson |
| 4 | Introduction | 1-2 paragraphs: why this case is unique |
| 5a | Patient Info | De-identified demographic info |
| 5b | Patient Info | Primary concerns & symptoms |
| 5c | Patient Info | Medical, family, psychosocial history; genetic info |
| 5d | Patient Info | Past interventions with outcomes |
| 6 | Clinical Findings | Significant PE and clinical findings |
| 7 | Timeline | Episode organized as timeline |
| 8a | Diagnostic Assessment | Testing (PE, lab, imaging, surveys) |
| 8b | Diagnostic Assessment | Diagnostic challenges |
| 8c | Diagnostic Assessment | Diagnosis (including differential) |
| 8d | Diagnostic Assessment | Prognosis (e.g., staging) |
| 9a | Therapeutic Intervention | Types (pharmacologic, surgical, etc.) |
| 9b | Therapeutic Intervention | Administration (dosage, strength, duration) |
| 9c | Therapeutic Intervention | Changes with rationale |
| 10a | Follow-up & Outcomes | Clinician & patient-assessed outcomes |
| 10b | Follow-up & Outcomes | Follow-up diagnostic/test results |
| 10c | Follow-up & Outcomes | Adherence & tolerability |
| 10d | Follow-up & Outcomes | Adverse & unanticipated events |
| 11a | Discussion | Strengths AND limitations of this case |
| 11b | Discussion | Relevant medical literature with references |
| 11c | Discussion | Scientific rationale for conclusions |
| 11d | Discussion | One paragraph: primary take-away lesson |
| 12 | Patient Perspective | 1-2 paragraphs from the patient |
| 13 | Informed Consent | Was consent obtained? |

## Part 3: Anesthesia-Specific Additions (based on SPS literature analysis)

> 以下为基于 SPS 文献分析补充的麻醉特异性条目，与 ACRE 第 7 项（Interventions）和第 9 项（Follow-up and outcomes）配合使用。

| # | Addition | Requirement |
|:--|:---|:---|
| A1 | Anesthetic Plan | Complete anesthetic plan: premedication, induction agents/doses, airway technique, maintenance, emergence |
| A2 | Monitoring | Specific monitors used (ECG, SpO2, NIBP/IBP, ETCO2, BIS, TOF, temperature) |
| A3 | Drug Timing | Chronological sequence of drug administration with doses |
| A4 | Airway Details | Difficult airway assessment, intubation grade, device type |
| A5 | Hemodynamic Course | Intraoperative hemodynamic trends |
| A6 | Recovery | PACU course, pain scores, PONV, time to discharge |
