### B6. SPS高频学术固定搭配（SPS-Specific Academic Collocations）

#### B6.1 疾病描述（Disease Description）

| English | 中文 |
|:---|:---|
| Stiff-person syndrome (SPS) is a rare autoimmune neurological disorder characterized by progressive muscle rigidity and episodic painful spasms. | 僵人综合征（SPS）是一种罕见的自身免疫性神经系统疾病，以进行性肌肉僵硬和发作性痛性痉挛为特征。 |
| SPS is typified by rigidity in muscles of the torso and extremities and painful episodic spasms. | SPS以躯干和四肢肌肉僵硬及发作性疼痛痉挛为典型特征。 |
| SPS is a rare autoimmune disease characterized by fluctuating rigidity and stiffness of the axial muscles. | SPS是一种罕见的自身免疫性疾病，以躯干肌波动性强直和僵硬为特征。 |
| The clinical spectrum of SPS ranges from classic axial rigidity to more severe phenotypes such as PERM. | SPS的临床谱系从经典型躯干僵硬到更严重的表型（如PERM）不等。 |
| SPS is frequently underrecognized due to overlapping musculoskeletal and neuropsychiatric symptoms. | 因与肌肉骨骼和神经精神症状重叠，SPS常被漏诊。 |
| The syndrome is caused by increased muscle activity due to decreased inhibition of the central nervous system. | 该综合征由中枢神经系统抑制减弱导致肌肉活动增加所致。 |
| Spasms can be triggered by sudden movements, emotional stress, and peripheral and auditory stimulation. | 痉挛可由突然运动、情绪应激、外周和听觉刺激触发。 |
| Stressors, such as fear, anxiety, and pain may trigger symptoms. | 恐惧、焦虑和疼痛等应激源可能触发症状。 |
| SPS is a progressive disease due to a deficiency in GABA synthesis. | SPS是一种因GABA合成不足导致的进展性疾病。 |
| Autonomic symptoms (tachycardia, hyperhidrosis, blood pressure changes, constipation, urinary retention) frequently accompany the spasms. | 自主神经症状（心动过速、多汗、血压变化、便秘、尿潴留）常伴随痉挛出现。 |

#### B6.2 病理生理（Pathophysiology）

| English | 中文 |
|:---|:---|
| The pathophysiology involves autoantibodies against glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD), the rate-limiting enzyme for GABA synthesis. | 其病理生理涉及抗谷氨酸脱羧酶（GAD）的自身抗体，GAD是GABA合成的限速酶。 |
| The pathophysiology centers on impaired GABAergic inhibitory neurotransmission. | 病理生理核心在于GABA能抑制性神经传递受损。 |
| Anti-GAD antibodies target the rate-limiting enzyme for GABA synthesis, resulting in motor neuron hyperexcitability. | 抗GAD抗体靶向GABA合成的限速酶，导致运动神经元过度兴奋。 |
| Dysfunction of GABA-mediated inhibitory networks in the central nervous system underlies the disease. | 中枢神经系统GABA介导抑制网络的功能障碍是该病的基础。 |
| Autoantibodies attack the GAD enzyme → impaired GABA synthesis → loss of GABAergic inhibition → motor neuron hyperactivity → progressive rigidity. | 自身抗体攻击GAD酶→GABA合成受损→GABA能抑制丧失→运动神经元过度活跃→进行性僵硬。 |
| Continuous motor unit activity (CMUA) at rest is the electrophysiological hallmark of SPS. | 静息状态下持续性运动单位活动（CMUA）是SPS的电生理特征。 |

| English | 中文 |
|:---|:---|

| A multidisciplinary strategy is essential for sustained functional recovery and quality of life in SPS. | 多学科策略对SPS患者持续功能恢复和生活质量至关重要。 |
| Prognosis is highly variable; some patients respond well to therapy while others experience progressive disability. | 预后差异很大；部分患者对治疗反应良好，而其他患者则经历进行性残疾。 |
| Long-term monitoring is essential given the chronic and relapsing nature of the disease. | 鉴于该病的慢性和复发性特点，长期监测至关重要。 |

#### B6.5 麻醉管理（Anesthetic Management）

| English | 中文 |
|:---|:---|
| Anesthetic management of patients with SPS presents unique challenges because of impaired GABAergic inhibition. | SPS患者的麻醉管理因GABA能抑制受损而面临独特挑战。 |
| Patients with SPS are at high risk for prolonged hypotonia and mechanical ventilation. | SPS患者存在长时间肌张力低下和机械通气的高风险。 |
| Caution should be exercised when using neuromuscular blocking agents in SPS patients. | 在SPS患者中使用神经肌肉阻滞剂时应谨慎。 |
| TIVA without muscle relaxants may be the preferred anesthetic technique for SPS patients. | 无肌松药的TIVA可能是SPS患者首选的麻醉技术。 |
| Heightened sensitivity to muscle relaxants has been documented in SPS. | 文献已记录SPS患者对肌松药敏感性增高。 |
| Conflicting reports exist regarding the safety of neuromuscular blocking agents in SPS. | 关于肌松药在SPS中的安全性存在相互矛盾的报道。 |
| Interaction between anesthetic agents and therapeutic drugs may cause prolonged effects. | 麻醉药与治疗药物（如巴氯芬、苯二氮䓬类）的相互作用可能导致作用时间延长。 |
| Prolonged weakness after general anesthesia may result from enhanced central GABAergic transmission due to the interaction between volatile agents and baclofen. | 全麻后长时间无力可能是挥发性麻醉药与巴氯芬相互作用导致中枢GABA能传递增强所致。 |
| We utilized a dexmedetomidine infusion for a smoother emergence to decrease SPS triggers. | 我们使用右美托咪定输注以实现更平稳的苏醒，减少SPS诱因。 |
| The literature shows that suitable conditions for tracheal intubation can be provided without the use of neuromuscular agents. | 文献表明，在不使用神经肌肉阻滞剂的情况下也可提供合适的气管插管条件。 |
| Regional anesthesia has been successfully used in SPS patients and is advocated as the safest mode. | 区域麻醉已成功用于SPS患者，并被提倡为最安全的麻醉方式。 |
| A patient showed postoperative hypotonia despite recovery from muscle relaxant on TOF monitoring. | 一名患者尽管TOF监测显示肌松恢复，但术后仍出现肌张力减退。 |
| Evaluating intraoperative depth of anesthesia and postoperative hypotonia using EEG monitoring and clinical assessment of muscle strength recovery in addition to TOF monitoring is crucial. | 除TOF监测外，利用术中脑电图监测和临床肌力恢复评估来评价术中麻醉深度和术后肌张力减退至关重要。 |
| Remimazolam could be considered an effective anesthetic drug for patients with SPS. | 瑞马唑仑可被视为SPS患者有效的麻醉药物。 |
| We think the TIVA technique, a general anesthetic practice which does not require neuromuscular blockage, is suitable for these patients. | 我们认为TIVA技术（一种不需要神经肌肉阻滞的全身麻醉方法）适用于此类患者。 |
| We present a successful endotracheal intubation and TIVA application without muscle relaxants in an SPS patient. | 我们报道一例SPS患者在不使用肌松药的情况下成功行气管插管并应用TIVA。 |
| The mechanism(s) mediating sensitivity to muscle relaxants in SPS is unknown. | 介导SPS患者肌松药敏感性的机制尚不明确。 |
| Using the smallest dose necessary of NMBAs is recommended when their use cannot be avoided. | 当无法避免使用NMBAs时，推荐使用最小必要剂量。 |
| Discussing the risk of postoperative ventilation with the patient prior to surgery is critical. | 术前与患者讨论术后通气风险至关重要。 |
| This case report is the first to establish the safe use of both depolarizing and non-depolarizing NMBAs in an SPS patient. | 本病例报告首次证实了SPS患者使用去极化和非去极化NMBAs的安全性。 |
| To the best of our knowledge, this is the first case report describing the use of remimazolam in a patient with SPS. | 据我们所知，这是首例描述瑞马唑仑用于SPS患者的病例报告。 |
| Our report is the first to document successful anesthetic management for robotic surgery in an SPS patient. | 本报告是首个记录SPS患者机器人手术成功麻醉管理的文献。 |
| General anesthetic management requires attention to autonomic symptoms such as delayed emergence, hypotonia, tachycardia, and hyperthermia. | 全身麻醉管理需注意自主神经症状，如苏醒延迟、肌张力减退、心动过速和高热。 |
| It is important that detailed disclosure be made in the preoperative period and sufficient sedation be ensured during the procedure. | 术前进行详细告知并在操作过程中确保充分镇静非常重要。 |
