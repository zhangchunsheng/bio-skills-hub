## Description: <br>
A month-by-month pregnancy reference skill covering preconception, prenatal nutrition, symptom navigation, medical tests, labor and delivery, breastfeeding, postpartum recovery, and partner support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heardlyapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/heardlyapp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Expectant parents, partners, and caregivers use this skill as a book-style pregnancy guide for understanding pregnancy stages, common symptoms, nutrition, prenatal visits, birth preparation, breastfeeding, and postpartum recovery. It is a reference aid and not a substitute for a qualified prenatal clinician. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat pregnancy guidance as individualized medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a book-style reference and confirm supplement use, abnormal symptoms, test results, labor concerns, delivery choices, and postpartum complications with a qualified prenatal clinician or urgent care as appropriate. <br>
Risk: The skill can proactively provide medical-style guidance without enough clinical context. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid relying on it for diagnosis, emergency triage, or treatment decisions; escalate concerning symptoms and clinical decisions to qualified care. <br>
