## Description: <br>
Searches a local Xiwang graphene medical research corpus and helps draft evidence-based medical video scripts from matching documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[ling61616161-dotcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/ling61616161-dotcom) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and content teams can search a named Xiwang medical research document corpus by keyword, topic, or disease name, then use the retrieved source material to prepare medical education or product-promotion video script drafts. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The search script can scan local folders, which could expose unrelated personal, corporate, or patient-data files if pointed at a broad directory. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with the intended Xiwang medical research corpus and avoid broad personal, corporate, or patient-data directories. <br>
Risk: Generated medical education or product-promotion copy may contain claims that require clinical, legal, or advertising review. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft research or marketing material, cite retrieved source documents, avoid cure claims, and complete clinical and legal review before publication. <br>
Risk: PDF text extraction can contain recognition errors that affect retrieved passages or downstream summaries. <br>
Mitigation: Verify important claims against the original source document before using them in public-facing content. <br>
