## Description: <br>
Helps users reflect on Ayurvedic constitution patterns and get low-risk nutrition, lifestyle, and wellness tips with medical safety boundaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for an informal Ayurvedic constitution reflection and for conservative food, lifestyle, and wellness suggestions. The skill frames results as non-diagnostic and directs users to qualified clinicians for medical concerns, pregnancy, pediatric nutrition, chronic conditions, medications, allergies, eating disorder recovery, or urgent symptoms. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake Ayurvedic constitution scoring or wellness suggestions for medical diagnosis or treatment. <br>
Mitigation: The skill must state that results are informal and non-diagnostic, avoid disease-treatment claims, and recommend qualified clinical care for symptoms, medical conditions, medications, pregnancy, minors, eating disorder recovery, allergies, medically restricted diets, or major nutrition changes. <br>
Risk: Users may ask for supplements, herbs, mineral products, detoxes, fasting, medication changes, or other higher-risk Ayurvedic interventions. <br>
Mitigation: The skill limits advice to ordinary food, sleep, hydration, movement, stress reduction, and seasonal comfort, and it avoids recommending herbs, minerals, cleanses, fasting, panchakarma, rasa shastra products, or medication changes. <br>
Risk: Ayurvedic medicines or imported products can create safety concerns, including possible heavy-metal exposure. <br>
Mitigation: The skill cautions against unverified products and directs users to clinicians, pharmacists, emergency services, or poison control when product safety, severe symptoms, or possible poisoning is involved. <br>
