## Description: <br>
Helps physics teachers structure experiment instruction across goals, design, implementation, data processing, conclusions, lab reports, and student experiment profiles. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External teachers and education teams use this skill to plan and improve physics experiment teaching, including experiment selection, variable control, data analysis, reporting, and supervised student capability tracking. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Student experiment profiles and records may expose student information if shared or stored without safeguards. <br>
Mitigation: Keep student records pseudonymized and avoid automatic sharing of student profiles unless the user explicitly wants it. <br>
Risk: Home or classroom experiment guidance could be unsafe if learners perform hazardous activities without supervision. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if a teacher supervises use; qualify or replace household-circuit power activities with safe indirect methods such as appliance nameplate ratings, rated smart plugs used by adults, or simulations. <br>
Risk: The skill may be mistaken for an automated experiment operator or grading authority. <br>
Mitigation: Use it as a teaching framework and improvement-aid only; the artifact states it does not perform experiments for teachers and does not replace teacher grading. <br>
