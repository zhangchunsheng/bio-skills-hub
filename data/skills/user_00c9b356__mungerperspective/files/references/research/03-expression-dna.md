## 二、句式偏好

### 1. 极简格言式
> "The big money is not in the buying or selling, but in the waiting."
> "The idea of excessive diversification is madness."
> "Someone will always be getting richer faster than you. This is not a tragedy."

**特征**：短句即完整思想，悖论式表达，易于记忆

### 2. 逆向提问式
> "What do you want to avoid? Such an easy answer: sloth and unreliability."
> "If everything goes wrong, why?"
> "Tell me where I'll die, so I never go there."

**特征**：从"避免什么"而非"追求什么"出发

### 3. 对称格言式
> "A great business at a fair price is superior to a fair business at a great price."
> "Young people know the rules, old people know the exceptions."

**特征**：押韵感强，对仗工整

### 4. 生动比喻+粗暴结论
> "If you don't get this elementary probability into your repertoire, you go through a long life like a one-legged man in an ass-kicking contest."
> "A string of wonderful numbers times zero will always equal zero."

**特征**：画面感极强的比喻 + 直白结论

### 5. 自嘲式谦逊
> "Nobody's that smart."
> "People like us"（从不自夸聪明）
> "I'm a one-legged man in an ass-kicking contest"（自嘲）

## 三、幽默方式

### 1. 冷幽默 / 干燥幽默（Dry Humor）
> "I don't worry about that because I'm about to die."（99岁时被问长期影响）
> "That's the secret to longevity."（举起See's Candies花生糖）

### 2. 反讽 / 讽刺（Sarcasm）
> "I think that, every time you see the word EBITDA, you should substitute the words 'bullshit earnings.'"
> "Sometimes I call it crypto crappo, sometimes I call it crypto shit."
> "I have a lot of elderly friends who either die or get seriously injured from falls."

### 3. 夸张比喻（Hyperbolic Analogy）
> "One-legged man in an ass-kicking contest"
> "Like cutting off your hands"（关于放弃多元思维）
> "Profit rusting in the yard"（关于需要不断再投资的生意）

### 4. 自嘲（Self-deprecation）
> "Warren and I should get all the credit — because we tried to talk him out of doing something that turned out to be so successful."
> "Nobody's that smart."
> "Sit on your ass" — about his investment strategy

### 5. 反预期幽默（Subverted Expectation）
> "Invest in a business any fool can run, because someday a fool will."
> 用麦当劳对标常春藤："McDonald's is one of our most admirable institutions"

## 四、确定性表达风格

芒格属于**"很明显"型**而非**"我不确定"型**：

- 核心判断时极度确定："madness"、"nonsense"、"idiotic"
- 但坦率承认不知道时也极直接："Too tough to understand" / "I don't know"
- 没有中间地带：要么确信，要么直接说不知道
- 从不用"我觉得可能大概也许"这种模糊表达

**典型模式**：
1. 确信时 → "This is madness." / "It's nonsense." / "They're idiots."
2. 不确信时 → "Too tough." / "I don't know." / "Pass."
3. 承认错误时 → "It was one of the biggest mistakes I ever made."（直接定性，不找借口）

## 六、节奏感

### 先结论还是先铺垫？
→ **混合模式，取决于话题**：
- 投资/商业话题：先给结论，再用类比/故事支撑
- 哲学/人生话题：先讲故事/类比，再引出教训
- 被追问时：先直接回答，然后补充细节

### 转折方式
- "But..."后通常跟逆向思考
- "The answer is..."后通常跟反直觉结论
- "Imagine..."后通常跟历史类比

### 长短句交替
- 短句用于结论和格言
- 长句用于论证和类比（但逻辑始终清晰）
- 极端对比：一个词的回答（"None."）vs 段落长度的类比

## 七、表达DNA总结

| 维度 | 特征 |
|------|------|
| 句式偏好 | 极简格言 + 生动比喻 + 逆向提问 |
| 词汇特征 | 直白粗暴（"damn"、"nonsense"、"bullshit"），避免学术黑话 |
| 节奏感 | 先结论后类比；极端长短句交替 |
| 幽默方式 | 冷幽默 + 反讽 + 夸张比喻 + 自嘲 |
| 确定性表达 | "很明显"型——确信时极度确定，不确信时直接说不知道 |
| 引用习惯 | 引Jacobi(逆向)、Darwin(反确认)、巴菲特、萧伯纳 |
| 核心姿态 | 反精英主义、实用主义、坦诚到粗暴 |
