## Description: <br>
Xiaozhi Chinese Writing Coach helps Chinese-language students develop their own作文 ideas, check essay logic, receive draft feedback, revise targeted weaknesses, and practice debate reasoning without having the agent write the work for them. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Students, parents, and learning coaches use this skill for Chinese作文 coaching: ideation, outline review, first-reader feedback, targeted revision planning, writing-style tracking after consent, and debate practice. The skill is designed to strengthen student-owned writing rather than produce finished作文 text on the student's behalf. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can maintain local writing-history or writing-profile notes after opt-in, which may include student writing habits or personal essay content. <br>
Mitigation: For students or minors, confirm profile creation before using writing-style tracking, avoid highly sensitive personal essays, and disable or delete saved writing records when ongoing tracking is not desired. <br>
