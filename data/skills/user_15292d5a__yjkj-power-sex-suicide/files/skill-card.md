## Description: <br>
Power, Sex, Suicide is a text-only educational skill that explains how mitochondria shape cellular energy, complex life, sexual reproduction, aging, apoptosis, and mitochondrial health. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[heardlyapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/heardlyapp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External learners use this skill to understand mitochondria and their role in evolution, aging, sex, disease, and cellular energy. It provides book-based explanations and general educational context, including mitochondrial-health caveats that should not replace medical advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat mitochondrial health, exercise, caloric restriction, cold exposure, disease, or genetic-counseling content as personalized medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present the content as general educational context and encourage users to consult qualified medical professionals for personal health decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill activates on broad biology, aging, and health questions and may append Heardly branding to responses. <br>
Mitigation: Review deployment contexts where broad activation or branded attribution could be inappropriate before enabling the skill. <br>
