## Description: <br>
Plan the retirement of a product feature, including the decision record, affected-user analysis, migration paths, communication timeline, data handling, and removal checklist. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, engineering leads, customer-facing teams, and operators use this skill to plan feature deprecation or retirement with explicit migration, communication, data-handling, exception, and removal steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may elicit sensitive business context, including customer usage, contracts, data retention duties, and code removal plans. <br>
Mitigation: Limit inputs to information appropriate for the agent session and review the generated plan before sharing or acting on it. <br>
Risk: A sunset plan can affect deletion, compliance, or customer communications if followed without review. <br>
Mitigation: Have responsible product, engineering, legal, support, or account owners validate deletion, retention, contractual, and communication steps before execution. <br>
