## Description: <br>
Lindsey Fitzharris's The Butchering Art is an executable toolkit that extracts lessons from Joseph Lister's effort to transform Victorian surgery through antiseptic technique and overcome entrenched opposition. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill to learn from The Butchering Art through Joseph Lister's antisepsis breakthrough, Victorian medical history, resistance to innovation, and practical lessons about persistence and evidence-led change. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger terms such as surgery or hygiene may activate the skill in unrelated medical or cleanliness conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Review the activation scope before installation and narrow triggers if the skill should only respond to book, Joseph Lister, antisepsis, or Victorian medicine contexts. <br>
Risk: The skill provides historical and innovation guidance rather than current medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use it for education and analogy; rely on qualified clinical sources for present-day medical decisions. <br>
