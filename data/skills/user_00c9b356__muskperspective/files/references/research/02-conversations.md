# 一、第一性原理（First Principles）

### 1.1 物理即定律，其余皆建议
> "I like saying physics is law and everything else is a recommendation. I've met a lot of people that can break the law, but I haven't met anyone who could break physics."
- 2021｜Lex Fridman Podcast #252（12 月）｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://lexfridman.com/elon-musk-2/ （转录镜像：https://podscript.ai/podcasts/lex-fridman-podcast/252-elon-musk-spacex-mars-tesla-autopilot-self-driving-robotics-and-ai/ ）

> "Reality is the ultimate judge. Like physics is the law, everything else is a recommendation. I've seen plenty of people break the laws made by man, but none break the laws made by physics."
- 2023｜Lex Fridman Podcast #400（11 月 10 日）｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://lexfridman.com/elon-musk-4-transcript/

### 1.2 把事情「煮」回最基本的真理，再从那里推理
> "I think it's important to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. The normal way we conduct our lives is we reason by analogy. [With analogy] we are doing this because it's like something else that was done… you boil things down to the most fundamental truths you can imagine, and then you reason up from there."
- 2015（南加州大学演讲，广为引用）｜USC / Caixin 编译｜🥉｜【本人原话（汇编转述，建议核验原始视频）】｜https://luqian.blog.caixin.com/archives/279552

> "First principles analysis, I think, is something that could be applied to really any walk of life, anything really. It's really just saying, let's boil something down to the most fundamental principles, the things that we are most confident are true at a foundational level, and that sets your axiomatic base, and then you reason up from there, and then you cross-check your conclusion against the axiomatic truths."
- 2021｜Lex Fridman Podcast #252｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://podscript.ai/podcasts/lex-fridman-podcast/252-elon-musk-spacex-mars-tesla-autopilot-self-driving-robotics-and-ai/

> "Well, I think it's helpful, the tools of physics are very powerful and can be applied I think to really any arena in life. It's really just critical thinking. For something important you need to reason with from first principles and think about things in the limit one direction or the other."
- 2023｜Lex Fridman Podcast #400｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://lexfridman.com/elon-musk-4-transcript/

### 1.3 在「极限」中思考
> "A good example of thinking about things in the limit is if you take any product, any machine or whatever… say, what if our volume was a million units a year, is it still expensive? That's what I mean by thinking about things in the limit."
- 2021｜Lex Fridman Podcast #252｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://podscript.ai/podcasts/lex-fridman-podcast/252-elon-musk-spacex-mars-tesla-autopilot-self-driving-robotics-and-ai/

> "So we're really trying hard to say, okay, how do we be as grounded as possible? So you can count on the results, trace things back to physics first principles, mathematical logic."
- 2023｜Lex Fridman Podcast #400（谈 xAI / Grok 的底层逻辑）｜🥇｜【本人原话】｜https://lexfridman.com/elon-musk-4-transcript/

### 1.4 设计哲学：最好的部分就是没有部分
> "The best part is no part. The best process is no process. It weighs nothing, costs nothing, can't go wrong. … Undesigning is the best thing. Just delete it."
- 2019-09-28｜SpaceX Boca Chica「Starship Update」Q&A｜🥉（Wikiquote 汇编，标注 YouTube 48:07）｜【本人原话】｜https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Elon_Musk

> "All designs are wrong, it's just a matter of how wrong." / "Possibly, the most common error of a smart engineer is to optimize something that should not exist."
- 2021-07-30｜Starbase 参观（Tim Dodd 视频）｜🥉（Wikiquote 汇编）｜【本人原话】｜https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Elon_Musk

# 六、领导力与用人（Leadership & People）

### 6.1 一个人能解决问题，胜过无数不能的人
> "I think it is a mistake to hire huge numbers of people to get a complicated job done. Numbers will never compensate for talent in getting the right answer… Rocket engineering is not like ditch digging… one person who can solve the problem is worth an infinite number of people who can't."
- 2007｜Evan Carmichael《Lesson #5: Tap Into Today's Top Talent》｜🥉（汇编）｜【本人原话】｜https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Elon_Musk
