## 4. Search Query Templates (搜索查询模板)

```
Query 1: (心电电极 OR 心电图电极 OR 心脏电极) AND (制作工艺 OR 生产工艺 OR 制造方法)
Query 2: (电极模块 OR 电极贴片 OR 电极扣) AND (制作 OR 生产 OR 制造) AND (方法 OR 工艺)

Query 5: (导电凝胶 OR 水凝胶) AND (电极) AND (心电 OR 心电图) AND (制作 OR 涂布)

```
Query 1: (ECG electrode OR EKG electrode OR cardiac electrode) AND (manufacturing OR fabrication OR production) AND (process OR method)
Query 2: (electrode module OR electrode pad OR electrode patch) AND (manufactur* OR fabricat* OR produc*) AND (method OR process)
Query 3: (wet electrode OR dry electrode OR solid-state electrode) AND (ECG OR EKG) AND (manufactur* OR fabricat*)

Query 5: (conductive gel OR hydrogel) AND (electrode) AND (ECG OR EKG) AND (manufactur* OR dispens* OR coat*)

## 6. Notes on Search Query Construction

- **Wildcards**: Use `*` for English word stems (e.g., `manufactur*` matches manufacturing, manufacture, manufactured)
- **Boolean operators**: `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` are supported by most databases. Use parentheses for grouping.
- **Chinese word segmentation**: Some databases (e.g., CNIPA) require exact Chinese phrases. Test both segmented and unsegmented forms.
- **Field codes**: Some databases support field-specific search (e.g., `TI=` for title, `AB=` for abstract, `IN=` for inventor, `PA=` for applicant). Check `references/patent_databases.md` for database-specific syntax.
- **Date ranges**: Most databases support date filtering. Common format: `20200101:20251231` or `2020-2025`.
- **Status filter**: Consider filtering by `GRANT` (granted) vs `APP` (application/pending) depending on analysis needs.
