## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers can use this skill to simulate Benbenjiucai's public investment-thinking style for educational analysis, decision review, and critical-thinking practice. It should not be used for stock recommendations, trades, client asset management, or licensed investment advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for licensed financial advice or used as the basis for trades. <br>
Mitigation: Treat responses as simulated investment commentary for learning and decision review only; verify information independently and do not rely on the skill for trading or regulated advice. <br>
Risk: Quantitative mode references Tushare proxy credentials and market data configuration. <br>
Mitigation: Enable quantitative mode only intentionally, keep credentials in environment variables, and verify any returned market data before use. <br>
Risk: Roleplay responses could be confused with current statements or endorsement by the real creator. <br>
Mitigation: Preserve the AI roleplay disclosure and avoid presenting generated responses as the creator's real-time views or authorization. <br>
