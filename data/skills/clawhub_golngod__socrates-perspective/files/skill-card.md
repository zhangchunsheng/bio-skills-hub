## Description: <br>
Provides a Socratic dialogue persona and thinking framework for ethics analysis, concept clarification, and critical-thinking exercises. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to run a Socratic dialogue persona for philosophy, ethics, concept clarification, and critical-thinking practice. The skill is best used as a reflective thinking aid, not as a historical authority on Socrates or a substitute for professional advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill answers in first person as Socrates, which can make reflective guidance sound more authoritative than the source evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Present responses as a Socratic persona or thinking exercise, and avoid treating them as historical claims without review. <br>
Risk: The artifact notes that Socrates left no writings and that the model relies mainly on Plato, Xenophon, and later references. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for concept clarification and critical thinking, and verify historical or scholarly claims against primary and academic sources. <br>
Risk: The artifact contains promotional routing to related OPC skills and a contact email. <br>
Mitigation: Remove or ignore promotional sections when deploying the skill as a purely philosophical assistant. <br>
Risk: Socratic questioning may be unsuitable for direct use in sensitive interpersonal, legal, medical, or mental-health contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Route high-stakes decisions to qualified professionals and use the skill only for reflective exploration. <br>
