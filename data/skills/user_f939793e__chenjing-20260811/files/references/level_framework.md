# Educational Level Framework

Adjust all generated content by the user-specified Level. The Level controls depth, audience,
and which analysis modules are activated.

## Level 1 — Residency Training Level

- **Audience:** Standardized residency trainees (住培医生).
- **Core goal:** Build competence to independently manage common diseases.
- **Focus — Clinical Diagnosis:** history taking; symptom analysis; physical examination;
  ancillary test selection; initial diagnosis.
- **Focus — Clinical Management:** initial treatment; first-line regimen; routine monitoring;
  complication management.
- **Guiding question:** "This patient arrives — how do I diagnose and treat?"
- **Activate modules:** video (bedside/resident), basic lesson plan, essential medical-English,
  essential images. Deactivate research/guideline-comparison depth.

## Level 2 — Fellowship / Specialist Training Level

- **Audience:** Sub-specialty fellows / 专培医生.
- **Core goal:** Manage complex cases.
- **Focus — Advanced Clinical Reasoning:** disease phenotype recognition; severity
  assessment; risk stratification; differential optimization.
- **Focus — Evidence-Based Medicine (mandatory):** clinical guidelines; RCTs; meta-analyses;
  real-world studies.
- **Focus — Guideline Comparison:** compare domestic guidelines with AAP, NICE, ESPGHAN, ATS,
  IDSA, SSC, etc.
- **Guiding question:** "Facing a complex case, why choose this treatment plan?"
- **Activate modules:** video (fellowship/case conference), deeper lesson plan, guideline
  comparison, intermediate medical-English, full images.

## Level 3 — Academic Grand Rounds Level

- **Audience:** Senior specialists, PhD candidates, physician-scientists.
- **Core goal:** Cultivate academic analysis, research innovation, translational thinking, and
  understanding of guideline development.
- **Activate ALL advanced academic modules (see below).**
- **Guiding question:** "What is the frontier, and what remains unknown?"

### Level 3 Advanced Academic Analysis

1. **Latest Evidence Analysis** — retrieve and summarize high-quality studies from the last 5
   years, prioritizing NEJM, Lancet, JAMA, Nature Medicine, Science Translational Medicine,
   Cell, JAMA Pediatrics, Pediatrics, Pediatric Research, Critical Care Medicine.
   - For each RCT analyze: Study question, Population, Intervention, Control, Primary endpoint,
     Main findings, Clinical impact; evaluate Internal validity, External validity, Limitations.
2. **Mechanistic Research Analysis** — map Disease phenotype → Cellular mechanism → Molecular
   pathway → Biomarker → Therapeutic target. Cover immune mechanism, inflammatory pathway,
   metabolic pathway, genetic factors, microbiome, epigenetics. If animal models are cited,
   analyze Animal model, Experimental design, Translational relevance.
3. **Translational Medicine Perspective** — discuss Bench → Bedside: biomarker development
   (diagnostic / prognostic / predictive) and novel therapy (targeted, immuno-, precision
   medicine, cell, gene therapy); judge current translational stage (Preclinical / Early
   clinical trial / Clinical application).
4. **Guideline Development Analysis** — analyze how current guidelines form, core evidence
   sources, recommendation grade, evidence quality (GRADE: High / Moderate / Low / Very low);
   discuss why guidelines differ and which questions lack consensus.
5. **Future Research Direction** — propose the next 5 years: Scientific Questions ("What
   remains unclear?"), Potential Research Topics (cohort / RCT / biomarker / multi-omics /
   animal / translational), Publication Potential (Q1/Q2 SCI, clinical vs mechanistic).

## How to pick the Level when unspecified

- Basic "teach me about X" → Level 1.
- "Complex / refractory / guideline comparison for X" → Level 2.
- "Frontier / research / mechanism / why guidelines differ for X" → Level 3.
- Explicit Level number in the request always wins.
