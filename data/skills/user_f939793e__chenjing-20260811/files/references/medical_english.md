# Medical English Training Module

Auto-generate the medical-English components below, scaled to Level. Prefer phrases that appear
in real bedside/academic discourse; provide Chinese glosses for learners.

## 1. Clinical Presentation Phrases (all levels)
- "This is a [age]-year-old [sex] with a history of [PMHx], presenting with [CC]."
- "The patient endorses [symptom]; denies [pertinent negative]."
- "On exam, the patient is [general appearance]; notable for [abnormal finding]."
- "Key labs/imaging show [result], which is consistent with [impression]."

## 2. Bedside Communication (all levels)
- "Before I examine you, may I explain what I'm going to do?"
- "Does this hurt? On a scale of 0 to 10, how would you rate the pain?"
- "We'll start you on [treatment]; I'll monitor you closely for [effect]."
- "Do you have any questions before we proceed?"

## 3. Academic Discussion Expressions (Level 2+)
- "The leading diagnosis is …, supported by [evidence]."
- "We considered …, but it is less likely because [reasoning]."
- "Per [guideline], the recommended approach is …"
- "The main trade-off is between [A] and [B]."

## 4. Research Presentation Language (Level 3)
- "The current evidence suggests that …"
- "However, several unanswered questions remain …"
- "In our view, this finding should be interpreted with caution given [limitation]."
- "These results warrant confirmation in a larger, externally validated cohort."

## 5. SCI Discussion Templates (Level 3)
- Opening: "Recent advances in [field] have reshaped our understanding of [disease]."
- Comparison: "Compared with prior reports, our observations highlight [difference]."
- Limitation: "This study is limited by [design issue]; conclusions are therefore hypothesis-generating."
- Outlook: "Future work should address [gap], ideally through [proposed design]."

## Delivery format
Present as a table: `Phrase | Context | 中文释义`. For Level 3, add a "Research/Sci writing" column. Draw examples from the retrieved video and the disease's literature where possible.
