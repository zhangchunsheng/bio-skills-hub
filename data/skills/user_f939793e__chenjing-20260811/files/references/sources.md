# Preferred Sources for Medical English Teaching Rounds

When searching for videos, prioritize the sources below. Verify each candidate video actually exists before recommending (do not invent links). Prefer official institutional channels and recognized medical education platforms.

## 0. Region & Accessibility Policy (DEFAULT: China / CN)

Many top English teaching-round videos live on YouTube / Vimeo / institutional sites that are
**blocked in mainland China**. To keep recommendations actually watchable:

- **Default region = CN.** Search domestic-accessible platforms FIRST and look for China mirrors
  of foreign content BEFORE falling back to blocked foreign links.
- **Always tag each result with an Accessibility flag** in the output (see `references/output_format.md`):
  `国内直连` (directly openable in CN) or `需科学上网` (needs a VPN/proxy).
- **Only include `需科学上网` links when** no domestic equivalent exists AND the user has not
  restricted to CN-only. Clearly mark them so the user is not surprised.
- For foreign-channel content, prefer its **re-uploaded version on Bilibili / 腾讯视频** (see §6).

## 1. Domestic-Accessible Sources (China) — search FIRST

These open without a VPN and should be the default recommendation pool:

- **Bilibili (B站)** — the primary hub for CN-accessible medical education. Search:
  `"[疾病] 教学查房"`, `"[疾病] bedside teaching rounds"`, `"[医院] 教学查房"`, and hospital
  official accounts (e.g., 复旦儿科、华西医院、协和、儿科学界、丁香公开课). Many Mayo / Johns
  Hopkins / Osmosis videos are re-uploaded here.
- **腾讯视频 / 西瓜视频 / 优酷** — hospital and medical-media uploads.
- **网易公开课 / 中国大学MOOC (icourse163)** — structured clinical courses.
- **丁香公开课 / 医学界 / 医脉通 / 梅斯医学** — Chinese medical-education platforms with case/round videos.
- **医学微视 / 各医院官方视频号、公众号** — search by hospital name + "教学查房"; links may be
  in-article embeds (note the source even if not a direct player URL).

## 4. Pediatric Priority Sources (when specialty = Pediatrics)

Prioritize pediatric-specific rounds over general adult rounds:
- **Pediatric teaching rounds** — search "pediatric teaching rounds", "paediatric bedside teaching"
- **NICU rounds** — neonatal intensive care teaching rounds
- **PICU rounds** — pediatric intensive care teaching
- **Pediatric emergency teaching** — PEM teaching sessions
- **Pediatric physical examination teaching** — pediatric clinical skills
- Channels: **St. Louis Children's Hospital**, **Boston Children's Hospital**, **Great Ormond Street (GOSH)** teaching content, **AAP** (American Academy of Pediatrics) videos.

## 5. Search Query Patterns (use WebSearch)

Combine a source hint with a round-type term and the target disease/specialty. **Run the
domestic queries first**, then the foreign ones only as a fallback.

Domestic-accessible queries (CN, run first):
- `"[疾病] 教学查房" site:bilibili.com`
- `"[疾病] bedside teaching rounds" bilibili`
- `"[医院名] 教学查房" site:qq.com OR site:bilibili.com`
- `"[疾病] 临床教学 视频" 丁香公开课 OR 医学界`

Foreign / mirror queries (run after, tag `需科学上网` unless a CN mirror is found):
- `"[disease]" "teaching rounds" OR "ward round" OR "bedside teaching" english`
- `"[specialty]" "clinical teaching rounds" site:youtube.com`
- `"case discussion" "[disease]" "differential diagnosis" medical education`
- `"[hospital]" "grand rounds" "[specialty]"`
- `"resident" "[disease]" "physical examination" teaching`

Mirror-discovery queries (find CN re-uploads of foreign content):
- `"[disease] teaching rounds" "哔哩哔哩" OR "bilibili"`
- `"Mayo Clinic [disease] 教学查房" bilibili`
- `"Osmosis [disease]" bilibili 中文`

Always confirm: (a) the video exists and is reachable, (b) the primary audio/language is English, (c) it shows real or simulated clinical teaching, not a pure lecture/slide talk. For CN links, also confirm the page actually plays (not a dead re-upload).

## 6. Level-specific search terms

Tailor queries to the requested Level (see `references/level_framework.md`). Prefer domestic
sources per §1; only escalate to foreign academic centers (tagged `需科学上网`) for Level 3
when no CN equivalent exists.

- **Level 1 (Residency):** `bedside teaching rounds`, `resident teaching`, `clinical clerkship
  [disease]`, `ward round [disease] physical examination`.
- **Level 2 (Fellowship/Specialist):** `fellowship rounds`, `clinical case conference
  [disease]`, `specialist teaching [disease]`, `grand rounds [disease] management`.
- **Level 3 (Academic Grand Rounds):** `academic grand rounds [disease]`, `expert clinical
  conference [disease]`, `multidisciplinary rounds [disease]`, `research seminar [disease]`.
  Prioritize top academic centers: Harvard Medical School, Johns Hopkins Medicine, Mayo Clinic,
  Stanford Medicine, UCSF, Yale Medicine — but still check Bilibili/腾讯视频 for CN mirrors first.

Combine with the disease and a source hint, e.g.:
`"septic shock" "academic grand rounds" "Johns Hopkins"`
