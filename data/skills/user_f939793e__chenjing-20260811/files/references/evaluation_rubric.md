# Clinical Teaching Quality Evaluation Rubric

Apply this rubric to every candidate video before recommending. Each dimension is scored 1–10; the final evaluation reports four sub-scores (Medical quality, Educational value, Research value, English learning value) plus a Teaching Level assignment.

## A. Medical Accuracy (Medical value)

Evaluate:
- **Clinical knowledge accuracy** — are the facts/pathophysiology correct for the disease?
- **Guideline consistency** — does management align with current major guidelines (e.g., AHA/ACC, IDSA, AAP, Surviving Sepsis)?
- **Evidence-based practice** — are recommendations supported by evidence rather than anecdote?

Score 9–10: accurate, guideline-concordant, explicitly evidence-based. Score 1–3: contains errors, outdated, or non-evidence-based.

## B. Teaching Quality

Evaluate:
- **Reasoning explained** — does the teacher articulate *why* (differential diagnosis, clinical decision making)?
- **Learner participation** — do students/residents ask or answer; is there interaction?
- **Clinical questions discussed** — are uncertainties, pitfalls, and teaching points addressed?

Score 9–10: strong Socratic teaching, active learners, clear takeaways. Score 1–3: passive slide narration, no interaction.

## C. English Learning Value

Evaluate:
- **Medical vocabulary richness** — frequency and appropriateness of clinical terms.
- **Doctor–patient communication examples** — real or simulated bedside dialogue.
- **Clinical presentation language** — structured case presentation (HPI, exam, assessment/plan).
- **Academic discussion expressions** — phrases for differential, grading certainty, proposing plans.

Score 9–10: rich, authentic English clinical discourse. Score 1–3: minimal spoken English or mostly visuals.

## D. Authenticity Classification

Assign exactly one category per video:
1. **Real bedside teaching rounds** — actual patient encounter / bedside teaching
2. **Simulated teaching rounds** — standardized patient / simulation lab
3. **Conference-based case discussion** — case conference / grand rounds (no bedside)
4. **Online medical education demonstration** — produced educational video (still valuable if criteria met)

## C2. Research Value

Evaluate (relevant especially for Level 2/3 requests):
- **Evidence linkage** — does the video reference guidelines, RCTs, or current literature?
- **Academic depth** — does it discuss mechanisms, controversies, or research frontiers?
- **Scholarly usefulness** — value for specialist/PhD training and research orientation.

Score 9–10: explicitly evidence-based, cites literature, discusses frontiers. Score 1–3: no research linkage.

## Teaching Level Assignment

Assign the highest Level the video effectively serves:
- **Level 1** — bedside/resident teaching, common-disease management.
- **Level 2** — fellowship/case conference, complex reasoning, guideline discussion.
- **Level 3** — academic grand rounds / multidisciplinary / research seminar.

## Teaching-Round Content Completeness (internal checklist)

Each recommended video should ideally include:
- **Patient Presentation**: chief complaint, HPI, PMH, medication history, family history, social history.
- **Clinical Reasoning Discussion**: differential diagnosis, diagnostic reasoning, clinical decision making, evidence-based management.
- **Physical Examination Teaching**: bedside examination, physical signs explanation, findings interpretation.
- **Management Discussion**: treatment strategy, monitoring plan, prognosis, follow-up plan.

A video lacking bedside examination but strong on reasoning may still qualify (category 3/4) — note the gap explicitly.

## Hard Rejection Rules

Do NOT recommend if any of these apply:
- Cannot confirm the video exists or the link is reachable.
- Primary language is not English (Chinese-narrated, or English subtitles only over non-English speech).
- Pure medical animation with no clinical teaching.
- Pure conference keynote / slide lecture with no case or bedside element.
- Clickbait / title-driven medical content with no educational substance.

If no video fully meets the standard, state clearly: "目前未找到完全符合标准的英文医学教学查房视频，以下推荐为最接近资源，并说明差距。" and list each candidate's gap.
