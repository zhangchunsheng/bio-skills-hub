# Delivery Checklist

- The merchant brief has business type, city, target customer, offer, pain, differentiator, and CTA.
- The current activity object is explicitly variable and includes service scene, target segment, goal, and confirmed offer details.
- For pet hospitals, the selected scene is one of vaccines, exams, deworming, spay/neuter assessment, senior-pet care, or a clearly named custom scene.
- The weekly theme is narrow enough to remember.
- Every public post points to one private action.
- Douyin local push, Meituan, Gaode, Moments, and private-domain handoffs have an owner and next action.
- Short-video scripts include hook, scene, proof, offer, CTA, and shooting notes.
- Xiaohongshu notes include local keywords and save-worthy details.
- WeChat Moments posts sound like a real shop owner, not an ad agency.
- Private-chat scripts cover inquiry, objection, reminder, and follow-up.
- Offer design does not use fake scarcity or unverifiable claims.
- KPI table tracks exposure, inquiries, appointments, arrivals, verifications, real reviews, revisits, revenue, and notes.
- Appointment, arrival, verification, review, and revisit records are not backfilled or conflated.
- Medical wording, case privacy/de-identification, price, inventory, and service promises have human approval.
- No fake reviews, fake slots, efficacy/cure promises, or coercive selling.
- Client delivery note explains what to review before posting.
