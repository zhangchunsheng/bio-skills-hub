# Growth Playbook

## Content Logic

Local-business content is not just "write posts". It should connect a variable activity object across a measurable journey:

1. Nearby trigger: why the customer needs this now.
2. Trust proof: what makes the shop believable.
3. Low-friction action: how to ask, reserve, visit, or try.
4. Follow-up: how the shop converts private messages into visits, verification, reviews, and revisits.

## Platform Roles

- Douyin / WeChat Channels: make the scene visible with a short story.
- Xiaohongshu: capture search and decision intent with local keywords.
- Douyin local push: amplify local reach and send qualified users to POI, offer, or inquiry.
- Meituan: capture nearby search, store-page decision, offer, appointment, and verification.
- Gaode: keep POI, route, phone, and opening information accurate for arrival intent.
- WeChat Moments: build trust through founder voice, real scenes, and limited trial offers.
- Community / private chat: close the loop with invitation, objection handling, and follow-up.

For pet hospitals, the object can switch between vaccines, exams, deworming, spay/neuter assessment, senior-pet care, and a merchant-defined service. Carry the same object through `触达 → 咨询 → 预约 → 到店 → 服务/核销 → 真实评价 → 复诊`.

## Safety Rules

Never recommend:

- Fake reviews, fake scarcity, fake queue screenshots, or fabricated customer stories.
- Medical, financial, legal, or exam-result guarantees.
- Platform rule evasion, mass spam, or scraping private data.
- Misleading discounts that are not actually available.
- Online diagnosis, prescriptions, dosage instructions, or efficacy/cure promises.
- Publishing an identifiable pet case without authorization and de-identification.

Use "test", "try", "experience", "availability confirmed by the merchant", and "human review required" language. Treat all prices, slots, service promises, and medical wording as review-gated inputs.

## Service Proof

For a paid delivery, include at least one:

- Before/after content sample.
- Folder screenshot of generated assets.
- 60-second screen recording.
- Merchant feedback quote.
- KPI table after a 7-day test.
