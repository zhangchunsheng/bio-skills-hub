## 🔍 Search Strategies

### For Symptom Analysis
1. **Start with Chinese resources** for Chinese-speaking users
2. **Cross-reference multiple sources** before making inferences
3. **Use specific symptom combinations** in searches
   - Example: "头痛 发热 咳嗽 可能疾病"
   - Example: "abdominal pain nausea diarrhea possible causes"

### Search Queries to Use
When searching for symptom analysis, use queries like:
- "[症状1] [症状2] 可能是什么病"
- "[symptom1] [symptom2] possible causes"
- "[symptom1] [symptom2] differential diagnosis"
- "Mayo Clinic [symptom] causes"
- "WebMD [symptom] symptoms"

### For Hospital Records / Medical Literature
- Search for: "临床症状 分析 总结" (clinical symptoms analysis summary)
- Search for: "[disease name] clinical presentation"
- Search medical case studies: "[symptom] case study"

## ⚠️ Source Quality Assessment

### ✅ Reliable Sources
- Hospital and medical institution websites
- Government health department websites
- Peer-reviewed medical journals
- Established medical organizations (Mayo Clinic, NHS, etc.)
- Medical school websites

### ❌ Less Reliable Sources
- Personal blogs or forums (use only for supplementing, not primary analysis)
- Unverified health websites
- Social media posts
- Commercial websites selling treatments or supplements

## 📋 Using web_fetch Tool

When using `web_fetch` to retrieve medical information:

1. **Fetch from authoritative sources** listed above
2. **Extract relevant information** about:
   - Possible conditions matching the symptoms
   - Common causes
   - Risk factors
   - When to see a doctor
   - Recommended medical specialty
3. **Cite the source** in your analysis
4. **Verify information** across multiple sources when possible

## Medical Specialties Reference

When recommending which department to consult, use this guide:

- **内科 (Internal Medicine)**: General symptoms, fever, cough, digestive issues
- **外科 (Surgery)**: Injuries, lumps, appendicitis symptoms
- **儿科 (Pediatrics)**: Children's diseases
- **妇产科 (Obstetrics & Gynecology)**: Women's health, pregnancy
- **心血管科 (Cardiology)**: Chest pain, heart-related symptoms
- **神经内科 (Neurology)**: Headaches, dizziness, numbness, stroke symptoms
- **消化内科 (Gastroenterology)**: Abdominal pain, digestive issues
- **呼吸内科 (Pulmonology)**: Cough, breathing issues, lung-related
- **皮肤科 (Dermatology)**: Skin rashes, lesions
- **眼科 (Ophthalmology)**: Eye-related symptoms
- **耳鼻喉科 (ENT)**: Ear, nose, throat symptoms
- **急诊科 (Emergency)**: Severe, acute, or life-threatening symptoms
