## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to produce LYGO-oriented file-integrity guidance, hash receipt workflows, memory archive compression plans, and seal or champion upgrade templates. It is intended as an advisor that runs only when invoked and pairs with a separate LYGO-MINT verifier for package verification. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The included self-check can be mistaken for strong security verification. <br>
Mitigation: Treat it as a legacy consistency check and use a dedicated verifier for package or lattice validation. <br>
Risk: Published anchors or archives may expose private information if users include sensitive content. <br>
Mitigation: Review archive and anchor content before publishing and omit secrets or private data. <br>
Risk: Legacy naming may make the skill sound broader than its actual advisory role. <br>
Mitigation: Present it as LYGO-specific file-integrity and provenance guidance, not as a medical, P0 byte-gate, or lattice-health verifier. <br>
