## Description: <br>
A Chinese-language physics lesson-planning skill that helps teachers build lesson frameworks around physics concepts, demonstrations, laws, models, applications, and classroom summaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Physics teachers and tutors use this skill to design structured physics lessons that introduce concepts through real-life situations, demonstrations, counterexamples, experiments, data analysis, modeling, and applications. It is intended to support teacher planning and classroom guidance rather than replace teacher judgment or lesson delivery. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Classroom experiment suggestions may not match a school's safety rules or available supervision. <br>
Mitigation: Review all experiment suggestions against local school safety policies before use, and avoid hazardous demonstrations. <br>
Risk: Student-profile notes can expose identifiable student information if stored without controls. <br>
Mitigation: Use pseudonyms where possible, collect permission before storing student data, and keep a clear retention plan. <br>
Risk: The skill may produce teaching frameworks that still require professional review for curriculum fit and accuracy. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as planning support and have the teacher adapt, verify, and deliver the final lesson. <br>
