## Description: <br>
Xiaozhi English Writing Coach helps learners improve English writing through grammar, vocabulary, and logic feedback, guided revision questions, sentence upgrades, scenario practice, and consent-based progress tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[qizhitang](https://clawhub.ai/user/qizhitang) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External learners use this skill to receive focused English writing coaching for essays, short passages, emails, and practical scenarios. The skill guides revision through targeted questions instead of directly rewriting the student's work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Consent-based progress tracking may retain writing history or learning records. <br>
Mitigation: Before enabling tracking or cross-skill updates, confirm where records are stored, what will be remembered, and how the learner can clear them. <br>
Risk: Writing feedback may be incomplete or pedagogically unsuitable for a learner's level. <br>
Mitigation: Review coaching suggestions before treating them as authoritative, and prioritize the skill's limited 2-3 issue feedback pattern over exhaustive correction. <br>
