## Description: <br>
Generates structured clinical case summaries or case presentations in SBAR, SOAP, standard clinical, or narrative formats for educational, documentation, and handover use. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Healthcare professionals, educators, and documentation authors use this skill to turn anonymized clinical case details into structured SBAR, SOAP, case report, MDT summary, or handover text. Outputs are for documentation and education and require qualified clinical review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Identifiable patient information could be included in prompts or generated summaries. <br>
Mitigation: Use only anonymized case details and remove names, birth dates, medical record numbers, addresses, and other identifiers before use. <br>
Risk: Generated clinical summaries may omit context or present misleading clinical guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Have a qualified healthcare professional review all output before documentation, handover, education, or care-related use. <br>
