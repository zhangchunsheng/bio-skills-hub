## Description: <br>
Author or edit custom HyperFrames compositions for longer or multi-scene videos, brand and sizzle reels, montages, static loops, title cards, footage remixes, and freeform builds when no specialized workflow fits. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, creators, and agent operators use this skill to plan, build, verify, preview, and optionally render HyperFrames video projects when a generic multi-scene or companion video workflow is needed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can create and modify HyperFrames project files and run HyperFrames CLI commands. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated project changes and rely on HyperFrames lint/check gates before previewing or rendering. <br>
Risk: Media or provider workflows may require sign-in, billing, or authenticated actions. <br>
Mitigation: Review provider authentication and billing prompts before continuing, and use offline provider paths when they satisfy the requested capability. <br>
Risk: Generated video output may miss the requested scope, brand treatment, or final intent. <br>
Mitigation: Use the plan, storyboard, validation, and final preview approval gates before rendering. <br>
